Inkorae is a handwriting and PDF annotation app for Android tablets. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short: Inkorae keeps your notes on your device. It has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no online features. The developer does not receive your notes, PDFs, pictures, settings or usage information.

1. Information the app handles, and why

Inkorae works only with content you create or choose to bring into it. It uses that content for one purpose: to show, edit, save, export and back up your own notes.

  • Notebooks, pages, drawings and typed text. Your handwriting (pen strokes, including pressure and position), shapes, typed text, notebook and folder names, covers, paper styles and page layout.
  • PDFs you import. When you import a PDF, the app keeps a copy inside its private storage so you can write on it. The PDF's file name is used as the notebook's title.
  • Pictures you insert. Pictures from the Android photo picker or taken with your camera app are copied into the notebook. They are reduced in size (longest side up to 2048 pixels) and saved again as a new image file. Camera and location details embedded in the original photo are not copied into the saved picture.
  • App settings. For example your theme, pen and toolbar choices, stylus settings and sounds.
  • Backups. If you choose "Back up everything", the app creates a backup file containing your library.

Inkorae does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or any account details.

2. Where the information is stored

Everything above is stored on your device, in the app's private storage: a local database, ink files, imported PDFs, pictures and settings. Android keeps this storage separate from other apps on a normal, unmodified device.

3. Importing files

You pick files with the Android system file picker or photo picker, or take a picture with your device's camera app. Inkorae receives only the items you select. It does not request general storage access, and it does not request the camera permission, because the photo is taken by your camera app.

4. Exporting files and backups

  • Exporting a PDF. When you export, Android asks where to save the file. The app writes the PDF only to the location you choose. The original PDF you imported is not changed.
  • Backup and restore. A backup is a single zip file with your whole library (notebooks, folders, PDFs, pictures and ink). You choose where it is saved. The app does not encrypt backup files, so anyone who can open the file can read your notes. Restoring from a backup replaces the notebooks on the device with those in the backup file. The app keeps the notebooks that were on the device aside, in its private storage, in case the restore goes wrong. That copy is deleted the next time you restore, or when you uninstall the app.
  • Android's automatic backup is turned off for this app. The app's notebooks are therefore not copied to your Google account by the app. They are backed up only if you create a backup file yourself.

Once a file is saved to a location you chose, such as a cloud drive app, that provider handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Camera photos and temporary files

Some temporary files are kept in the app's private cache while you work:

  • Camera photos. When you add a picture with the camera, your camera app saves the photo to a temporary file first. Inkorae deletes that file once the picture is in your notebook, or if you cancel, no camera app is found, or adding the picture fails. If the app is interrupted before it can do this, it removes any leftover camera photo that is more than one hour old the next time you start the camera or open the app.
  • Cover thumbnails. These are small cached pictures of your notebook covers. Android may clear them at any time, and the app redraws them when needed.

6. Does Inkorae send information off your device?

Inkorae does not send your information to the developer or to anyone else.

  • The app does not declare Android's network permission, and its code contains no networking, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, login or payment features.
  • The app does not share your information with third parties.
  • Inkorae uses open-source software libraries for local functions: Android Jetpack libraries, a local database, and PdfBox-Android for handling PDFs. They run on your device. Inkorae does not use them to upload anything.
  • Inkorae does not write diagnostic logs and does not send crash reports. Your device's operating system, or settings you have turned on such as usage and diagnostics reporting, may collect crash information independently of Inkorae. Inkorae does not control that.

Files leave the app only when you choose to save or share them, as described in section 4.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Your content stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Deleting a notebook moves it to the Trash. Items in the Trash are deleted for good after 30 days, or immediately if you delete them permanently or choose "Empty Trash".
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private data from your device, including notebooks that are not backed up elsewhere, and the copy kept aside after a restore.
  • Files you exported or backed up are outside the app. Delete them yourself from the place where you saved them.
  • The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing on a developer server to delete.

8. Security

Your notes are kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps on a normal, unmodified device. However:

  • The app does not add its own encryption to notes or backups.
  • Protection depends on your device. Use a screen lock and keep Android updated.
  • Backup files and exported PDFs are ordinary files. Protect and store them with care.
  • No method of storage is completely secure, and the developer cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Inkorae is a general-purpose note-taking app. It does not collect or send personal information from any user, including children. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or online features. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about the app, please contact the address below.
